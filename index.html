Why invest?

Saving sets money aside. Investing puts it into assets that may grow in value, while also carrying risk.

Compounding means earning growth on your original money and on earlier growth. Given time, that can help growth build.

It is not a promise of profit. Values can fall, and some investments take time to turn into cash.

Small steps count

Start by learning what you can afford to set aside. Your goals and comfort with risk can guide what you explore.

Beginner questions

Start with simple definitions before comparing investment choices.

How much should I invest first?

Begin with an amount you can set aside without risking essential needs. A range such as Rs 1,000–5,000 can help you start learning.

What is diversification?

Diversification means spreading money across different asset types. It may reduce dependence on one outcome, but it does not remove risk.

Should I invest before I save?

Keep a separate emergency fund for urgent costs first. An emergency fund is money held for unexpected needs.

What should my time horizon be?

Your time horizon is how long you expect to keep money invested. Short goals may need easier access than long goals.

What does risk mean for me?

Risk is the chance that an investment changes value or loses money. Choose a level you can accept and keep learning about each option.
